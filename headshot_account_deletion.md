# Account Deletion – AI Headshot Studio: AI Photo

**AI Headshot Studio: AI Photo** allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

---

## How to Request Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion using **either** of the following methods:

### Option 1: In-App Deletion
1. Open the **AI Headshot Studio: AI Photo** app  
2. Go to **Settings Tab**  
3. Select **Delete Account**  
4. Confirm the request  

### Option 2: Email Request
Send an email to **komal.kukadiya92@gmail.com** with the subject line:

**Account Deletion Request – AI Headshot Studio**

Please include:
- Your registered email address or login method  
- Your name, if available  
- Any details that help us identify your account  

---

## Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon successful verification, the following data will be permanently deleted:
- Account-related user data  
- Name and profile information, if provided  
- Uploaded photos or face images, if stored  
- AI-generated headshot images, if stored  
- App-related usage data associated with your account, if applicable  

---

## Data Retention Policy

- Account and personal data will be deleted **within 7 days** of receiving a valid deletion request.  
- Certain data may be retained if required by **legal, regulatory, security, payment, or fraud-prevention obligations**, as permitted by law.
